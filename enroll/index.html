Membership terms

Tillbrook Rx Savings is a prescription discount program, not insurance, and the discount you receive depends on the medicine, the quantity and the pharmacy you choose. Discounts cannot be combined with insurance payments for the same prescription. Membership begins with a 30-day introductory period, after which it renews automatically at $39.95 per year until you cancel. You may cancel at any time, and a membership cancelled within its first 30 days is not charged. Prices at the counter are set by the pharmacy at the time of purchase.
